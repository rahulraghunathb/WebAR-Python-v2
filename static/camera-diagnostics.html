How To Read The Result

Awaiting Camera Test
  1. Test this page first at the same distance and lighting as the printed target.
  2. Then open the AR page and repeat the same pose.
  3. If this page is sharp but immersive AR is soft, the issue is the browser or device WebXR passthrough path.
  4. If both are soft, the issue is browser camera quality, focus distance, lens cleanliness, or lighting.

Why This Matters

  • Image tracking depends on high-frequency detail like printed edges and textured corners.
  • Soft optics reduce feature quality before the tracker even starts matching.
  • A blurry passthrough can make a correct model pose look unstable because the target never solves cleanly.

What We Can And Cannot Control

  • On this page we can request the rear camera and inspect browser-exposed settings.
  • In immersive WebXR we generally cannot force autofocus or focus distance the way we could with a custom media stream.
  • If this page looks sharp, tracker work should focus on pose solving and calibration rather than optics.

Known Good Test Setup

  • Keep the printed target roughly 25 to 40 cm away.
  • Let the target fill about one-third to one-half of the frame.
  • Use bright diffuse light and avoid glossy reflections.
  • Clean the camera lens before comparing pages.
